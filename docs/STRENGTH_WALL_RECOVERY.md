# Approved neural wall recovery

The human approved 18,000 additional wall seconds after a battery sleep consumed
the original twelve-hour wall allowance. This changes no engine, network,
checkpoint, mixture, opening, resource comparison or required game count.

`tools.strength_wall_recovery` first runs a metadata watcher. The original owner
keeps its deadline. Recovery starts only after its actual wall failure, closed
active ledger and exclusive-owner exit. Its separate immutable scope starts at
the original deadline, so a late start loses wall allowance. Original active
seconds, attempts and allocated bytes remain charged against the same combined
43,200 active seconds, three attempts and 20 GiB additional allocation.

Recovery pins committed game, pair and PGN bytes before resuming the archived
original arena. Individual committed games are reused even when their containing
pair record was interrupted. Temporary journal bytes are retained separately.
All earlier pinned bytes are checked again before accepting the full 4,000-game
sample. The original failure and scope are never rewritten as successful.

Independent replay and the original neural contribution endpoint remain
mandatory. A failed scientific endpoint stays failed. Either scientific outcome
can hand off to the original archived external owner for its separately bounded
2,000-game sample. Abnormal execution stops rather than silently trying again.
The selected model and original learning receipt remain unchanged.

The small hosted controls exercise budgets, ownership, interrupted-ledger
accounting, original-game reuse and byte preservation. They do not certify a
strength sample or the whole project.
