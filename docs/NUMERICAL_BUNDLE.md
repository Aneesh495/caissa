# Full numerical domain replay

G06 uses the `numerical-probes-v1` adapter. Its `numerical` object references
six distinct declared artifacts: the frozen numerical `profile`, producer
`config`, producer `summary`, raw `positions`, raw `observations` and selected
float `checkpoint`. Manifest hashes, the actual deployed executable/network,
validation-selected checkpoint identity, complete100000-probe sample, seed,
unchanged32cp bound and unclamped error contract must agree before execution.

The q4096 numerical profile additionally retains its original v3-float-to-v4
same-parameter export contract. The separate strength profile is unchanged.
Reusing an immutable mathematical specification does not renew its closed
campaign or authorize rerunning its old owner.

Admission is never a numerical pass. The adapter stages pinned bytes outside
the original evidence and starts the independent numerical reader in an owned
process group. That reader reconstructs the seeded legal walk, evaluates the
actual float checkpoint through its separate FP64 graph, interprets integer
network bytes and executes the saved native image. No training or model
selection occurs. The original full-bundle wall/active deadlines cover staging,
checkpoint loading and the entire child. Bounded output and native-process
group inheritance prevent an expired outer replay leaving native work behind.

A fully reconstructed error-bound failure preserves its observed distribution
and remains a failed G06 endpoint. The coordinator may continue to later
domains. Other process, provenance or artifact failures reject the replay.
Neither case can become a cached numerical success.

The hosted admission controls rehash14 corruptions and reach the actual full
CLI. Their metadata fixture intentionally lacks numerical work, so even its
valid admission certifies no probe sample. Full-size positive replay of a
complete source-bound campaign remains separate. Existing closed precision
campaigns, their negative results and the active match actors stay unchanged.
