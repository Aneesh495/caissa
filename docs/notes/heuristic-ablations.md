# Search switch ablations

The frozen [protocol](../../config/heuristic-study-v1.json) asks what each selective heuristic changes at two node budgets and two time budgets. The reference enables all ten required switches. Each other mode disables exactly one. The original classical evaluator, one thread, 16 MiB table, root ordering and unrelated switches stay fixed. No model, mixture or baseline tuning uses these results.

The [collector](../../tools/heuristic_study.py) completed 1056 queries: eight fixed positions, eleven modes, 5000/25000 nodes or 25/100 ms, and three repeats. A predetermined shuffled schedule reduces ordering effects. Every query clears the table. Actual options, raw UCI lines, request timestamps, incomplete iterations and legal fallbacks are retained in [the evidence](../../results/heuristic-study-2026-10-25/matrix/SUMMARY.json). Original full-model training was active on the same host, so these are loaded-host observations. They cannot establish isolated speed or playing strength.

The [independent verifier](../../verification/heuristic_study.py) parses raw responses through its separate transport/parser, recomputes all 44 aggregate groups, checks the exact schedule and option matrix, and reruns all 528 fixed-node queries with the externally pinned executable. Non-timing results and work counters must match. Timed observations remain measurements rather than deterministic replay targets. The verifier also checks disabled-heuristic counters, node ceilings, legal PVs, mate-in-one claims and actual elapsed timestamps. It rejected twelve [rehashed corruptions](../../results/heuristic-study-2026-10-25/controls/CONTROLS.json), including an internally consistent false native score that required executing the saved engine to disprove.

At 25000 nodes, the reference mean completed depth was 13.875. Switching LMR off reduced it to 13.125, history off to 13.375, and PVS off to 10.625. Removing extensions raised it to 14.375. Those means span unlike positions, including a simple forced mate, and do not rank decision quality. PVS-off changed nine of 24 best moves, while quiescence-off changed six. SEE-off and PVS-off each produced three legal fallbacks with no completed iteration; these remain in the means with depth zero. No failed or inconvenient row was removed.

At 100 ms the reference mean depth was 16.75, LMR-off 15.375, PVS-off 14.5, and extensions-off 17.8333. The changed decisions and depth shifts motivate dedicated tactical and match investigations. They do not establish that any off switch improves or hurts Elo. In particular, a higher depth with quiescence or extensions removed does not represent the same evaluated tree.
