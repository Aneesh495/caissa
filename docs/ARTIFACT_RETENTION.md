# Artifact retention

On October 27 and 28, 2026, the maintainer requested minimal local retention and
stopped bulk cloud archiving. Generated training corpora, obsolete checkpoints,
scratch builds, downloaded work files and the dedicated private Drive archive
were intentionally retired. Older transport receipts describe reconstruction at
the time of those runs. Their Drive IDs no longer provide a current restore path.

The checkout retains source, configuration, tracked compact results, the required
runtime, frozen inference executable and network, and the whole original parallel
sample, now closed with 1,110 attempt records and 555 pair records. Its initial
262 records and 131 pairs were preserved unchanged.

The original campaign floating-checkpoint path is unavailable, but the selected
C76 checkpoint still exists in the tracked `results/precision-v4-inputs/selected.pt`.
A current byte check confirmed all 9,518,976 bytes match SHA256
`c76d8e9ef0f2255531bc9085f5d0330a7a4152cf9b21a65ff8aee740ab8b2005`
and the [original selection identity](../results/precision-v4-inputs/IDENTITY.json).
This was an existing retained copy, with no acquisition, retraining or selection
change. Its availability does not restore the retired bulk corpora or obsolete
checkpoints.

Historical domain results remain historical results. This retention decision
does not make incomplete gates pass, provide public raw-data reproduction, or
permit retraining and model selection from old outcomes. Complete raw historical
reconstruction from this checkout is unavailable.

The original parent deadline expired on October 28 at 01:18:17 UTC. At that point,
no fifth parallel owner was admitted and 938 attempts were unrecorded. That
expired scope was not renewed. All original failures and cumulative limits
remain unchanged.

Later on October 28, the maintainer explicitly authorized continued work and
restored the follow-up. A new finite continuation records that authority
separately from the expired parent. The new storage boundary has
[source-bound synthetic integration evidence](../results/parallel-storage-continuation-2026-10-28/README.md).
The separately admitted fifth owner then reached the original cumulative active
limit and closed with 1,110 of 1,200 original attempts recorded. Five comparisons
were independently checked; the last comparison remains incomplete. No further
parallel start or active time is admitted. The follow-up remains active for
other authorized work. Historical raw-artifact retirement and the frozen actor
remain unchanged. Full acceptance and release remain blocked.
