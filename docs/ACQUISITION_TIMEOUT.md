# Acquisition confirmation failure

The searched-teacher corpus did not complete its frozen acquisition protocol. The three-hour final confirmation window expired after the producer had stopped adding pairs. The producer retained exit 2 with `preparation-confirmation-timeout`. The consumer subsequently screened the remaining journals, then exited because no successful acquisition receipt existed. `DATASET.json` and training arrays were not exported.

The final consumer checkpoint contains 114791 pairs, 229582 completed games and 16522987 raw positions. Its operational screened counts are 10578434 training inputs, 1336496 validation inputs and 1301363 test inputs. These exceed the numerical data floors, but operational counters alone do not establish independently replayed full acceptance. The producer's last observed checkpoint had 114300 pairs screened. Its original source, executable, seeds, search budget and complete game journals are retained.

The original 24-hour wall origin and 180000-second cumulative child CPU ceiling are unchanged. Actual reaped child CPU across generation sessions is 56848.35 seconds. Elapsed wall and monotonic durations differ substantially on this host. They are recorded separately and neither is relabeled as CPU time. The failed three-hour confirmation window remains exhausted even when the later consumer checkpoint reaches the complete prefix.

`training/corpus.py` now checks prior session budgets before scanning completed journals or starting new workers. It rejects a prior exhausted wall, child CPU, output or confirmation ceiling. A restart cannot enlarge an earlier finite maximum, remove it, undo an earlier tightening or reduce the disk reserve. A confirmation deadline is journaled once and reused after interruption. Expiry is checked before accepting a late consumer checkpoint.

Fifteen limit controls cover removed/enlarged ceilings, cumulative CPU, elapsed wall time, reduced reserve, repeated confirmation and prior hard stops. The actual failed full corpus was presented to the revised CLI: it refused before adding a session or game, and its configuration, session journal and failure receipt hashes remained unchanged. A separate small original searched acquisition completed, recorded its confirmation deadline and passed independent whole-dataset replay. These are recovery-control tests, not a pass for the failed full run.

A read-only byte audit subsequently matched every completed journal to the preserved pre-restart SHA256 manifest. The audit copier overwrote its process-owner CONFIG and progress filenames with source-corpus receipts. That diagnostic layout error is disclosed in `OWNER_CONFIG_COLLISION.json`; the actual command journal retains the 300-second owner ceiling and real exits. No full acceptance relies on that directory layout.

A separate six-hour fixed-prefix finalization budget has since been authorized and frozen. [The finalization workflow](FIXED_PREFIX_FINALIZATION.md) keeps the failed acquisition unchanged. The recovery freezes the existing 114791-pair prefix, adds no games, retains the original failure and exports the already committed preparation transaction. It then independently reconstructs every original game, split, feature, target and contributor. All existing data floors, held-out exclusions, searched-depth requirements and subsequent learning/strength requirements remain mandatory. The original proposed file stays inactive; the new finalization protocol is a separate record.

[Failure logs, immutable pair identities and guard controls](../results/acquisition-timeout-2026-10-23/) retain the raw observations. The machine-readable [proposed recovery](../config/data-finalization-v1.proposed.json) is deliberately inactive. Full training and decisive strength matches have not started.
